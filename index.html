Gongfan Fang

Research Scientist · NVIDIA

I'm Gongfan Fang, a Research Scientist at NVIDIA, where I'm working on LLMs. I completed my Ph.D. at the xML Lab, National University of Singapore, under the supervision of Prof. Xinchao Wang. Prior to that, I received my B.Eng. (2019) and M.Eng. (2022) from Zhejiang University under the supervision of Prof. Mingli Song.

My research focuses on Efficient Deep Learning. I am the creator of Torch-Pruning, an open-source framework for structured pruning and acceleration. And I also received the 2024 ByteDance Scholarship.
